How it works

  1. Enter your details and save them.
  2. The app checks your CGPA, income and category against the sample rules.
  3. Save a scholarship or add it to your application list.
  4. Update the status when you start or submit an application.

Your details stay in this browser. A sample match does not guarantee an award.
